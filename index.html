Packing, shipping and lead times

    

      
Packed in Houston every week
We pack requests in weekly batches and ship them every Friday, so allow up to a week for yours to go out.

      
Request by these dates for holiday delivery
International: Monday, November 30.
United States: Friday, December 11.
After that we'll keep shipping while supplies last, but yours may arrive in the new year.

      
Time in transit
United States: usually 2–5 business days after it ships. International: usually 1–3 weeks, and customs can add more. Local customs may charge import fees on arrival, which are outside our control.

      
Tracking
You'll get an email with a tracking number when your package ships. Outside the US, we need your phone number for customs.
